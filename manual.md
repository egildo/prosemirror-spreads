# The Manual (mock)

The confident description of using prosemirror-spreads, written before the thing exists so reality has something precise to disagree with during coding sessions. Requisites plus falsifiable coolness, nothing else.

**The genre rule:** every "how cool this is" sentence must be concrete enough to *lose*. "Select two columns and hit `=` to sum them" can disagree with reality; "editing feels great" cannot, so it may not appear here. A sentence that turns out false is the instrument working, not a bug in the manual.

This document is a stub, on purpose: chapters get written confidently when their ground has had its round, and each unwritten chapter says so.
